Privacy

Effective August 20, 2026

Information collected

Sandboxed stores your email address, membership status, registered device names, playback-session activity, and payment status. To limit repeated trials and account sharing, Sandboxed also creates protected hashes derived from device characteristics and IP addresses. Raw passwords are handled by the authentication provider and are not stored by Sandboxed.

Payments

Card information is processed by Stripe. Cryptocurrency payments are processed by NOWPayments. Sandboxed receives transaction identifiers and payment status, but does not receive complete card numbers or wallet recovery phrases.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, provide trials and memberships, enforce device and playback limits, process payments, prevent abuse, and provide support.

Retention and security

Active account records are retained while needed to operate the membership. Completed playback-session records are deleted after 30 days, revoked-device records after 90 days, and payment-event records after 400 days, subject to any longer legal requirement. Protected trial-abuse hashes may be retained after account deletion so deleting an account does not create another free trial. Technical server errors may be reported to Sentry after email addresses, account identifiers, and secret-like values are removed. Administrative credentials remain server-side, database tables use restricted access rules, and payment notifications are signature-verified.

Your choices

You may request account-data access or deletion through the support contact shown in your account. Some payment records and non-reversible abuse-prevention hashes may need to be retained for fraud prevention, accounting, or legal compliance.
